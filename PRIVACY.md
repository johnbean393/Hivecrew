# Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Hivecrew and Hivelink, including the source code, related apps, and supporting services in this repository.

## What We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information when you use Hivecrew or Hivelink:

- Account information, such as your email address.
- Authentication data, such as verification requests, session tokens, and device registration records.
- Device and service identifiers, such as push notification tokens, tunnel identifiers, cluster identifiers, and app bundle identifiers.
- User content, such as task prompts, task metadata, task status, agent questions, uploaded attachments, generated outputs, and session traces.
- Audio, video, or screen-sharing content, if you choose to use voice, camera, or screen broadcast features.
- Diagnostic and operational data, such as logs, timestamps, error details, and service health information needed to operate the product.

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- authenticate users and maintain account sessions;
- deliver product features, including remote access, task orchestration, notifications, voice sessions, widgets, and live updates;
- operate and secure backend services;
- troubleshoot failures and improve reliability;
- comply with legal obligations and enforce product policies.

## Third-Party Services

Hivecrew and Hivelink may send data to third-party providers that are necessary to deliver core functionality. Depending on the features you use, those providers may include:

- Cloudflare, for Worker, tunnel, DNS, and edge infrastructure;
- Resend, for verification email delivery;
- Apple services, for push notifications, widgets, and related platform features;
- model or AI providers configured by the app or user for voice and task execution features.

Your use of those services may also be subject to the privacy policies and terms of those providers.

## Data Retention

We retain data for as long as needed to operate the product, provide requested functionality, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Some locally stored data can be cleared from within the app. Account deletion is intended to remove account-linked remote access configuration and associated service records under our control, though backups and operational logs may persist for a limited period.

## Your Choices

You can choose whether to enable features that use:

- microphone access;
- camera access;
- screen broadcast or screen sharing;
- push notifications.

You can also delete your account from within the app when that feature is available in the shipped build.

## Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Children

Hivecrew and Hivelink are not intended for children under 13.

## Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes may be reflected in the app, the service, or this repository.
